What makes every investigation defensible.

Case Analysis Cockpit

Unified workspace for evidence exploration, timeline analysis, entity pivots, and finding management — all in one interface.

Chain of Custody

Evidence registration with cryptographic hashing, atomic writes, and full audit trail from collection through reporting.

HMAC Approval Boundary

Challenge-response authentication for finding approval. The AI never approves — the examiner signs every finding.

Three Investigation Modes

Mode 1: examiner-led exploration. Mode 2: LLM-guided iteration. Mode 3: agentic MCP with examiner approval at every step.

Playbook-Driven Needles

22 investigation playbooks suggest search needles per evidence family — IOCs, technique names, event IDs — with one-click search.

Transparency Ledger

Hash-chained audit log of every action — case sealing, finding approval, agent execution. Tamper-evident by design.
